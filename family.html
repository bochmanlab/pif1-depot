Family overview

All entries on one scale, drawn on one scale. The PIF1-like helicase domain is shared across members; the N-terminal regions (for example the PH-like domain in animal and yeast Pif1) and the flanking regions differ.

Inclusion rule. A protein is listed when UniProt cross-references it to PANTHER family PTHR47642 (PIF1-like helicases) and the entry is Swiss-Prot reviewed. The rule is applied by a script, so every entry shows which family it matched. See About for what the rule misses.

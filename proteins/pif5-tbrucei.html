Function

DNA-dependent ATPase and 5'-3' DNA helicase required for the maintenance of mitochondrial (kinetoplast) genome stability. Involved in processing of minicircle Okazaki fragments.

Catalytic activityCouples ATP hydrolysis with the unwinding of duplex DNA at the replication fork by translocating in the 5'-3' direction.
ATP + H2O = ADP + phosphate + H(+)
Subunit / complexMonomer
Disruption phenotypeEssential, it cannot be deleted
FamilyBelongs to the helicase family. PIF1 subfamily

Function

DNA helicase that stimulates viral DNA replication and recombination. Plays a role in T4 DNA replication initiation by selecting and activating DNA origins. Acts by dissociating and reassociating with the DNA molecule being unwound. Unwinds DNA as a monomer in a 5'-3' direction at a rate of 250 bp/s and can efficiently displace proteins from the DNA (PubMed:17823128, PubMed:22504228).

Catalytic activityCouples ATP hydrolysis with the unwinding of duplex DNA at the replication fork by translocating in the 5'-3' direction.
ATP + H2O = ADP + phosphate + H(+)
Subunit / complexMonomer (PubMed:12411580). Interacts with UvsX and gene 32 protein (PubMed:1328208). Interacts with the single-stranded DNA-binding protein; this interaction probably allows Dda to stimulate branch migration (PubMed:39417586)
